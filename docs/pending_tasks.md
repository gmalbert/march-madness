## 2. Betting Features & Extended Functionality

* ✅ **ROI tracker** – `roi_tracker.py` implemented with `ROITracker` class, bankroll history, win-streak tracking, monthly breakdown, and best-threshold finder.  Results persisted to `data_files/roi_tracker.json`.
* ✅ **Export picks** to CSV/JSON/HTML/PDF – `export_picks.py` auto-extracts value picks from `upcoming_game_predictions.json`; outputs go to `data_files/exported_picks/`.
* ✅ **Hyper‑parameter tuning** – `model_analysis.py --tune` runs RandomizedSearchCV (20 iters) over XGBoost param grid; saves `data_files/models/best_hyperparams.json` and `tuned_*_xgboost.joblib`.
* ✅ **Model versioning & A/B testing** – `model_analysis.py --compare` pits Model A (no weighting), B (3× tournament weighting), and C (saved tournament model) on 2024–2025 hold-out; results in `model_ab_test_results.json`.
* ✅ **Confidence intervals** – `generate_predictions.py` now computes ±1σ ensemble spread for spread, total, and moneyline predictions and writes `spread_ci`, `total_ci`, `moneyline_ci` fields to output JSON.

## 3. Tournament‑Specific Capabilities

* ✅ **Train tournament‑specific models** – `train_tournament_models.py` trains 9 models (XGBoost/Ridge/RF for spread/total, XGBoost/LR/GB for moneyline) with 3× tournament weighting and LOVO cross-validation.  Results saved to `data_files/models/tournament_model_results.json`.
* ✅ **Integrate tournament models** – `generate_predictions.py` now detects `season_type == "tournament"` and routes to tournament-specific models with graceful fallback.
* **Live tournament bracket updates** – automated Selection Sunday bracket fetching. (`roadmap-tournament-data.md`) — *still pending*

## 6. Feature Engineering & Efficiency Integration

* ✅ **Retrain models with extended features** (KenPom + BartTorvik) – tournament models trained on 18 enriched features; comparison shows dramatic gains vs 3-feature baseline.
* ✅ **A/B test CBBD‑only vs CBBD + external ratings** – covered by `model_analysis.py --compare`; saved to `model_ab_test_results.json`.
* ✅ **Feature importance analysis** – `model_analysis.py --importance` computes XGBoost gain-based and permutation importance for all 3 model types; saved to `feature_importance.json`.
  * Top spread feature: `spread_margin_diff` (59.5% gain, permutation 3.13 ± 0.07)
  * Top total feature: `total_combined_ppg` (43.6% gain, permutation 2.24 ± 0.06)
  * Top moneyline feature: `spread_margin_diff` (43.4% gain)
