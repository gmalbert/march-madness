# Bracket Oracle — 6-Month Feature Roadmap

## Month 1: Selection Sunday

- **Bracket viewer** — Visual 68-team bracket with model win probabilities for every potential matchup.
- **Seed comparison table** — All 68 teams sorted by composite efficiency rating with seeding comparison.
- **First Four predictions** — Win probabilities and DraftKings odds for the four play-in games.
- **Bubble watch tracker** — Teams on the bubble with at-large bid probability.

## Month 2: Tournament Week

- **Round-by-round tracker** — Live bracket updating with actual results; model recalculates after each round.
- **Upset alerts** — Notify when a top-5 seeded team loses.
- **Daily picks summary** — Today's best-value bets from the DraftKings tournament line.
- **Upset probability by seed line** — Historical 5/12, 6/11, 7/10 upset rates displayed prominently.

## Month 3: Analytics

- **Team deep-dive** — For any team in the bracket: KenPom AdjO/AdjD, BartTorvik Barthag, schedule strength, road win record.
- **Conference comparison** — Efficiency ratings averaged across each conference.
- **Historical simulation accuracy** — Model accuracy from prior tournaments by round.

## Month 4: Bracket Game Tools

- **Pool strategy advisor** — Given a bracket pool scoring system (e.g., 1/2/4/8/16/32), recommend optimal upset picks.
- **Contrarian pick finder** — Picks that are under-owned in public pools but have model support.
- **Simulation breakdown** — Top-10 national champion picks with probability ranges.

## Month 5: Pre-Season Features

- **Conference tournament predictions** — Model conference tournament winners to refine seeding predictions.
- **KenPom trend tracker** — Weekly efficiency rating movements for bubble teams.
- **Season-long power rankings** — Rolling 4-week composite efficiency rankings.

## Month 6: Automation

- **Selection Sunday email** — Full bracket predictions sent automatically.
- **Daily tournament email** — Day-of game picks with best bets highlighted.
- **GitHub Actions** — Auto-fetch KenPom and BartTorvik data weekly during season.
